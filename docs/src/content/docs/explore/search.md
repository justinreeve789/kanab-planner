---
title: Search
description: Find any trail, restaurant, event or place by name.
---

Open search with the magnifying glass in the header, or **Search** in the bar at the bottom of the screen on a phone.

1. Type part of a name, for example "narrows" or "pizza". Results appear as you type.
2. Narrow the results with **Select Areas** and the resource filter (trails, restaurants, events, locations).
3. Select a result to expand it and see its details and buttons.

Results are ordered by how closely they match what you typed. Events only appear if they haven't happened yet.

Each result is labelled with its area. Expand it to see:

- **Trails**: distance, climb, description, **View map**, **Ask question** and **Add to itinerary**.
- **Restaurants**: description, **Website**, **Ask question** and **Add to itinerary**.
- **Events** and **locations**: area, description and **Add to itinerary**.

:::tip
Searching from the questionnaire's Destinations step (**Find a trail** or **Find an event**) adds what you pick to your wish list rather than to a particular day.
:::
