Choose **Trails** in the header (or in the ☰ menu on a phone).

![The Trails page](../../../assets/screenshots/browse-trails.png)

With the search box empty, the page shows a few featured trails. Type to search by name, and use **Select Areas** to limit the results to one or more areas.

## What a trail shows

- **Distance** in miles. For out-and-back trails this is the round trip.
- **Climb**: the total elevation gained, in feet.
- **Average grade**: how steep the trail is on average, as a percentage.
- **Difficulty**: from Easy to Very Difficult, as published for the trail.
- A **description**, and photos where available.

On your itinerary, a hike also shows your estimated time for it, based on your [hiking pace](/docs/planning/preferences/#hiking-pace).

## Buttons

- **View map** shows the trail's route over a topographic satellite map. Zoom and pan to see the terrain around it.
- **Ask question** opens the [virtual assistant](/docs/explore/assistant/) with this trail selected, so you can ask about it directly.
- **Add to itinerary** adds the trail to a day of your current trip. Pick the day from the list; days in the trail's area are marked **nearby**. If you don't have a trip you can edit, it goes on your wish list for the next trip you create.

## Before you go

Trip Planner's trail information is a planning aid, not a guarantee. Conditions change with weather and seasons, and some trails need permits (Angels Landing and The Wave among them). Check with the land manager or park before you set out, carry plenty of water and tell someone your plans.
