title: Frequently asked questions
description: Quick answers to common questions about Trip Planner.
---

## Do I need an account?

No. You can plan, edit and share trips without one. An account lets you edit your trips from any device and keeps them in [My trips](/docs/accounts/my-trips/).

## I can't edit my trip any more

You're probably in a different browser or device, in a private window, or you cleared your browser data. Editing needs the browser that made the trip, or the account it's saved to. If you saved it to your account, [sign in](/docs/accounts/sign-in/). If you didn't, you can still view it with the link, but you'll need to build a new trip to make changes. See [Sharing and who can edit](/docs/itinerary/sharing/).

## The hike times look too long (or too short)

Hike times come from your hiking pace and each trail's climbing. Change your minutes-per-mile on the questionnaire's Activities step, then choose **New itinerary**. You can also [change an item's time](/docs/itinerary/editing/#change-an-items-time) directly.

## Why didn't I get any hikes?

Check that **Hiking** (or another trail activity) is ticked on the Activities step. With low adventurousness only about a quarter of your full days include a hike. Raise it for more. Arrival and departure days never include hikes.

## Why didn't I get any events?

Events are only added when adventurousness is at least halfway, you've chosen at least one event activity, and something is happening in that area on that day. You can always add an event yourself from the [Events page](/docs/explore/places/#events).

## Why is the North Rim missing from my trip?

The Grand Canyon's North Rim is closed from December 1 to May 14, and trips in that period don't go there. Low adventurousness also keeps day trips close to Kanab. See [Destinations and areas](/docs/planning/areas/).

## Why does a day say "Pack a lunch"?

You'll be out on a trail over lunchtime, or there's nowhere suitable to eat nearby, so Trip Planner reminds you to bring food and water.

## Can I stay somewhere other than Kanab?

Yes. On the questionnaire's first step, under **Where are you staying?**, choose any of the seven areas. You can also tap the map where you're staying and give it a name, which makes drive times more accurate. See [Where you stay](/docs/planning/areas/#where-you-stay).

## Why do drive times say "about"?

"About" means the drive time is an estimate from the straight-line distance, because the route couldn't be worked out from the road map for that drive. Times without "about" are worked out along the actual roads. See [Drive times and road alerts](/docs/itinerary/drives/).

## The weather isn't showing

Forecasts appear about a week ahead of each date. For trips further out, check closer to the time. Weather is shown on larger screens only.

## I'm told to wait before creating another itinerary

To keep things fast for everyone, you can build up to ten itineraries a minute. Wait a moment and try again.

## I forgot my password

Choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and we'll email you a link to choose a new one. See [Forgot your password?](/docs/accounts/sign-in/#forgot-your-password). If you used **Continue with Google**, you can also just sign in with Google again.

## Is the trail information official?

No. Trip Planner gathers trail, restaurant and event information from public sources to help you plan. Always check current conditions, closures and permit rules with the park or land manager before you go.
