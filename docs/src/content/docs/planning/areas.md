Trip Planner plans around **Kanab, Utah**. You stay in Kanab, arrive and leave from Kanab, and head out on day trips. Each full day of your trip belongs to one area, shown in the day's heading and on the day buttons.

## The areas

| Area | What it's known for |
| --- | --- |
| **Kanab** | Your base. Slot canyons, sand dunes, the Wire Pass and Buckskin Gulch area, and the town's restaurants and events. |
| **Zion** | Angels Landing, The Narrows, Observation Point and the park shuttle. Long Zion hikes are scheduled early to beat the crowds. |
| **Bryce Canyon** | Hoodoo amphitheaters and rim trails at high elevation. Expect cooler temperatures. |
| **Grand Canyon (North Rim)** | Quieter than the South Rim, with forested rim trails and big viewpoints. Seasonal, see below. |
| **Page, Arizona** | Horseshoe Bend and Antelope Canyon tours. |
| **Lake Powell** | Water, beaches and canyon views. |
| **St. George, Utah** | Snow Canyon, red-rock trails and a bigger choice of restaurants. |

## How areas are chosen

- Arrival and departure days are always in Kanab.
- Any area you asked for, by adding one of its trails, events, restaurants or places to your wish list, gets at least one day.
- The remaining days are picked at random, favouring the most popular areas. The more adventurous you are, the more evenly the less-visited areas get a turn, and the farther from Kanab you'll go. See [Adventurousness](/docs/planning/preferences/#adventurousness).
- If you've chosen hiking, an area is only picked if it has trails that suit your settings.
- Days in the same area are kept together, so two Zion days come back to back. The order of the groups is random, except that an event on your wish list moves its area's day to the date the event happens.

## Driving

Each away day shows the drive from Kanab, for example "49 min drive each way". The drive there and back appears on the day's timeline, and every plan has you back in Kanab by 9:30 PM.

## Seasonal closures

The Grand Canyon's North Rim is closed to visitors from **December 1 through May 14**. If any part of your trip falls in that period, the North Rim isn't used for that trip, even if you put one of its trails on your wish list.

:::caution
Roads, trails and park services can close at short notice for weather, fire or maintenance. Always check the park's official alerts before you set out.
:::
