Trip Planner plans around where you stay: **Kanab, Utah** unless you choose somewhere else. You arrive and leave from there and head out on day trips. Each full day of your trip belongs to one area, shown in the day's heading and on the day buttons.

## The areas

| Area | What it's known for |
| --- | --- |
| **Kanab** | The usual base. Slot canyons, sand dunes, the Wire Pass and Buckskin Gulch area, and the town's restaurants and events. |
| **Zion** | Angels Landing, The Narrows, Observation Point and the park shuttle. Long Zion hikes are scheduled early to beat the crowds. |
| **Bryce Canyon** | Hoodoo amphitheaters and rim trails at high elevation. Expect cooler temperatures. |
| **Grand Canyon (North Rim)** | Quieter than the South Rim, with forested rim trails and big viewpoints. Seasonal, see below. |
| **Page, Arizona** | Horseshoe Bend and Antelope Canyon tours. |
| **Lake Powell** | Water, beaches and canyon views. |
| **St. George, Utah** | Snow Canyon, red-rock trails and a bigger choice of restaurants. |

## Where you stay

Choose where you're staying on the questionnaire's [When & Where step](/docs/planning/questionnaire/#where-youre-staying). Any of the seven areas can be your base.

- **Arrival and departure days** are in your base. You check in on the first day and check out on the last.
- **Check-in** is at your lodging: "Check in at Cliffrose Lodge" if you named it, or "Check in at your lodging" otherwise. In Kanab, if you didn't name your own, it's at Trip Planner's listed lodging.
- **Drives** start and end at your pin, if you dropped one, or in the middle of your base area otherwise. A pin has to be within 50 miles of the area you chose.
- **Nearby areas** (within about 35 miles of your base) can come up as often as your base does. From Kanab that's Zion; from Page it's Lake Powell.
- **Seasonal closures** apply to your base too. You can't stay at the North Rim between December 1 and May 14. If you chose months rather than dates, Trip Planner picks dates when your base is open.

## How areas are chosen

- Arrival and departure days are always in your base.
- Any area you asked for, by adding one of its trails, events, restaurants or places to your wish list, gets at least one day.
- The remaining days are picked at random, favouring the most popular areas. The more adventurous you are, the more evenly the less-visited areas get a turn, and the farther from your base you'll go. See [Adventurousness](/docs/planning/preferences/#adventurousness).
- If you've chosen hiking, an area is only picked if it has trails that suit your settings.
- Days in the same area are kept together, so two Zion days come back to back. The order of the groups is random, except that an event on your wish list moves its area's day to the date the event happens.

## Driving

Every drive is on the timeline: from where you're staying to each stop in turn, and back again. Each day's heading shows its total driving, for example "2 h 10 min of driving". Trip Planner aims to have you home by 9:30 PM. See [Drive times and road alerts](/docs/itinerary/drives/).

## Seasonal closures

The Grand Canyon's North Rim is closed to visitors from **December 1 through May 14**. If any part of your trip falls in that period, the North Rim isn't used for that trip, even if you put one of its trails on your wish list.

:::caution
Roads, trails and park services can close at short notice for weather, fire or maintenance. Always check the park's official alerts before you set out.
:::
