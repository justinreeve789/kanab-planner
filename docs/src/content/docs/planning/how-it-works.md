---
title: How itineraries are built
description: The rules Trip Planner follows when it lays out each day.
---

You don't need to know any of this to use Trip Planner, but it helps explain why a day looks the way it does.

## The shape of a trip

1. **Day 1 is arrival.** Check in at your lodging around 4:00 PM, then dinner.
2. **The last day is departure.** Breakfast, then check out at 10:00 AM.
3. **Every day in between is a full day** in one area: your base or a day trip. See [Destinations and areas](/docs/planning/areas/).
4. Some full days are **trail days**. How many depends on your [adventurousness](/docs/planning/preferences/#adventurousness), and any day in an area with a wish-list trail is always one.

## The shape of a full day

Each day runs on a timeline. Nothing overlaps, and time to drive to the day's area and back is set aside first.

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| 7:00 AM | Breakfast at home, then the drive out. Adventurous travellers sometimes get breakfast at a café in the area instead. |
| Morning | Wish-list events and trails go in first, then anything else. Long hikes (six hours or more) and hikes of 2½ hours or more in Zion start in the morning. A long wish-list hike in Zion starts at 8:00 AM to beat the crowds, and breakfast moves earlier to make that work. |
| 11:30 AM – 1:30 PM | Lunch at a restaurant in the area if one is open and you're free. On a day at your base, lunch at home. If you're out on a trail, a **Pack a lunch** reminder appears at noon. |
| Afternoon | Mid-length hikes (three to six hours) can start in the morning or afternoon. Events and a wish-list place to see (about 90 minutes) fit around them. |
| 4:30 PM – 7:00 PM | Short, easy trails under 1½ hours make good sunset walks and are scheduled for the evening. |
| 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM | Dinner, usually at a restaurant, at home if nothing suits. |
| By 9:30 PM | Back where you're staying. On a day trip, dinner finishes in time for the drive home. |

Hikes only start when there's enough time to finish them and get home. If a hike won't fit, Trip Planner tries another or leaves the time free.

## Fitting in the drives

Once a day is planned, every drive in it is worked out along the roads, stop by stop: from where you're staying to the first stop, between stops, and home again. Then the day is adjusted so each drive fits.

- **Leave earlier.** If the first drive takes longer than planned, breakfast moves earlier (never before 6:00 AM) so you still arrive on time.
- **Start later.** If there isn't enough time to drive between two stops, the later one moves back. Events and check-in keep their times; if you'd be late, the drive says so.
- **Drop a stop.** If you still wouldn't get home by 10:00 PM, the day's last hike, event or place is dropped. Meals and anything on your wish list are never dropped, and a stop is only dropped if that actually gets you home sooner.
- **Bring dinner forward.** If dropping a stop leaves a late dinner at home, it moves earlier (but not before 5:30 PM).
- **Say when you'll be back.** If a day still ends after 9:30 PM, the drive home shows when you'll get back.

Road closures and roadwork on the day's drives are taken into account too. See [Drive times and road alerts](/docs/itinerary/drives/).

## Choosing what goes in

For each slot, Trip Planner looks for things that are:

- **in the day's area**;
- **open** at that time (restaurant and place opening hours are checked; places without listed hours are assumed open 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM);
- **happening that day**, for events;
- **not already in your trip**, so you won't see the same trail twice;
- **closest to your preferences** across adventurousness, solitariness and strenuousness.

## How long things take

- **Hikes** use your [hiking pace](/docs/planning/preferences/#hiking-pace) and the trail's elevation profile.
- **Restaurant meals** take about 45 minutes for breakfast, an hour for lunch and 1½ hours for dinner. They take longer at places that are usually busy at that hour.
- **Meals at home** take 30 minutes for breakfast, 45 for lunch and an hour for dinner.
- **Events** run for their listed times, up to three hours. Events without an end time are assumed to last four hours, or until 10:00 PM.

## Free days

If a full day at your base ends up with nothing but meals, it's marked as a day to relax. Use it to explore at your own pace, or [add your own plans](/docs/itinerary/editing/#add-your-own-item).
